Welcome to the Grand Stage

An Opportunity to Showcase Talent & Passion

TALENTRIA 26 is Akshara Vidyaashram's marquee open talent competition, specially designed for school children from Grades 1 through 9. It is a celebratory platform where confidence takes flight, artistic passions are celebrated, and every child discovers their moment in the spotlight.
